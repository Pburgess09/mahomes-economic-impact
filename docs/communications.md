# Communications Guide

## Recommended headline

**The salary is visible. The value chain behind it is much larger.**

## Short graphic copy

### With NFL Player

- NFL Team revenue: **$20.42 per second**
- NFL Team schedule broadcast-value proxy: **$19.82 per second**
- NFL Player-linked licensed products: **$2.38-$2.54 per second**
- Local bars and restaurants: **$0.79-$1.90 per second**
- Regional economic activity: **$31.49 per second**
- NFL Player compensation: **$1.80 per second**

### Without NFL Player

- Estimated quarterback compensation savings: **$1.48 per second**, assuming a $10M replacement.
- Estimated NFL Team revenue loss: **$2.03-$2.98 per second**.
- Estimated broadcast-value reduction: **$2.98-$4.95 per second**.
- Estimated licensed-product reduction: **$1.36-$2.06 per second**.
- Estimated restaurant and bar reduction: **$0.25-$0.48 per second**.

## Recommended close

> The NFL Team could save money at quarterback. They would likely give back more in revenue, attention, merchandise demand, and postseason economic activity. NFL Player is expensive because the market around him is valuable.

## Language controls

Use:

- "helps drive"
- "economic activity"
- "broadcast-value proxy"
- "modeled estimate"
- "commercial name, image, and likeness value"

Avoid:

- "NFL Player personally generates every dollar"
- "Home Market profits $993.2M"
- "networks make $625M in profit"
- adding overlapping categories into one exact total
- describing scenario ranges as audited figures
